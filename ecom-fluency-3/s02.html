Remember · words that work together

Say it · give a brief, set limits, refine

1. Give the task

Write ___ for ___.

Write three Instagram captions for first-time buyers.

2. Set a requirement

Make sure it ___.

Make sure it mentions the launch discount.

3. Set a limit

Keep it under ___.

Keep it under forty-five words per caption.

4. Name a source and a boundary

Use ___, and avoid ___.

Use the product brief, and avoid unsupported claims.

5. Request a focused revision

Revise ___ so that ___, while keeping ___.

Revise the opening so that it feels more practical, while keeping the offer.

Use the base verb for an instruction: Write / Use / Avoid / Revise. After Make sure it, use a complete clause: mentions, sounds, includes. “Under 45 words” means 44 words at most.

Word bank: and · for · keeping · so · sure · under

  • Write three captions first-time buyers.
  • Make it mentions the offer.
  • Keep it forty-five words.
  • Use the brief, avoid unsupported claims.
  • Revise the opening that it sounds more practical.
  • Shorten the caption while the call to action.

Speakingcomplete

Structured speaking

20 min

Hear how a real briefing conversation develops, then give the instructions yourself. The dialogue has twelve exchanges and keeps one campaign running from problem to revision.

Practice brief · fictional product

LumaAir Daily Gel Moisturiser

Approved facts
50 ml · lightweight gel · fragrance-free · absorbs quickly
Audience
Busy office workers trying LumaAir for the first time
Goal
Encourage visits to the product page
Offer
10% off until Sunday
Deliverable
Three English Instagram captions in a numbered list
Limits
Under 45 words per caption, including the CTA; no emojis or hashtags
Voice
Friendly and practical
Required ending
Shop the launch.

These are practice facts. Add no performance promises, clinical results, scarcity claims, or extra offers.

Model dialogue · fix the brief before the copy

Claire, a marketing lead, and Nathan, an e-commerce specialist, refine an AI prompt for a fictional LumaAir moisturiser launch. They need usable Instagram captions, not vague or exaggerated copy.

Open the dialogue transcript

ClaireThe AI captions sound polished, but none of them are usable. What did you ask for?

NathanJust, 'Write an exciting caption for our new moisturiser.' I thought it would infer the rest.

ClaireThat's the problem. Start with the task and the target audience.

NathanWrite three Instagram captions for busy office workers trying LumaAir for the first time. Is that specific enough?

ClaireBetter. Add the context: we want people to visit the product page, not necessarily buy immediately.

NathanI'll include the approved facts: it's a fifty-millilitre, lightweight, fragrance-free gel moisturiser that absorbs quickly.

ClaireGood. Make sure it mentions the ten percent launch discount and says the offer ends on Sunday.

NathanWhat about the brand voice? The current drafts sound like they're shouting.

ClaireMake sure it sounds friendly and practical, not overexcited.

NathanKeep it under forty-five words per caption. Does that include the call to action?

ClaireYes, everything. Use the product brief, and avoid unsupported claims.

NathanSo the line about erasing wrinkles overnight has to go. That isn't in the brief.

ClaireExactly. Add one reference example, but tell it to imitate the tone, not copy the wording.

NathanHere's one: 'Lightweight care for a busy day. Explore a simple addition to your routine.'

ClaireThat works. What output format will make the drafts easy to compare?

NathanA numbered list of three captions. Each one should end with 'Shop the launch.'

ClaireNow the second version is closer, but the opening still reads like a product catalogue.

NathanRevise the opening so that it speaks to a busy morning, while keeping the offer and call to action.

ClaireGood. Change that part only, so we don't lose the other constraints.

NathanAnd for the next iteration, I'll compare the new opening with the old one and check the word limit again.

ClaireWhat are our acceptance criteria?

NathanThree captions, under forty-five words each, approved facts only, the correct offer, a friendly voice, and the exact call to action.

ClaireGreat. Save the prompt and the chosen draft together, so the team can reuse the brief.

NathanGot it. I'll review every claim and count the words before anything goes live.

Until the recording is added, read in pairs or read both roles aloud. Then explain which prompt detail made the biggest difference.

Why was Nathan’s first prompt too weak?

The first prompt only asked for an exciting caption. The brief did not yet specify who, why, or what must stay within bounds.

Which offer must the captions communicate?

The approved offer is ten percent off until Sunday. No other discount or delivery promise appears in the brief.

What output does Nathan ask for?

The count, structure, and exact call to action are explicit.

What should the next revision change?

Nathan narrows the revision to the opening. The other constraints still apply.

Use the five chunks in order. Say your sentence before you type. Then repeat with your own product.

Suggested reflex answers
  1. Write three subject lines for returning customers.
  2. Make sure it mentions the launch discount.
  3. Keep it under forty-five words per caption.
  4. Use the approved product brief, and avoid invented benefits.
  5. Revise the opening so that it sounds more practical, while keeping the offer.

Combine the task, audience, facts, voice, limits, and output format. Use at least three of the five chunks.

Compare with one possible prompt
Write three English Instagram captions for busy office workers trying LumaAir Daily Gel Moisturiser for the first time. The goal is to encourage visits to the product page. Approved facts: 50 ml, lightweight gel, fragrance-free, absorbs quickly. The offer is 10% off until Sunday. Make sure each caption sounds friendly and practical and mentions the offer and its end date. Keep it under 45 words per caption, including the CTA. Use the approved facts, and avoid unsupported claims, emojis, and hashtags. Use this reference example for tone only; do not copy it: 'Lightweight care for a busy day. Explore a simple addition to your routine.' Return a numbered list of three captions. End each one with 'Shop the launch.'

A prompt makes the request clearer; you still need to check the resulting copy.

Speakingcomplete

Simulation

25 min

Run a briefing, challenge a weak draft, then respond to a tighter word limit. Your job is to make the next action clear and keep the agreed facts intact.

Round 1 · marketer and creative partner

Role A · Marketer

Use the LumaAir brief. Ask for three captions and explain the audience, offer, voice, source, and limits. Do not read your prompt word for word.

Role B · Creative partner

Ask: “Who exactly is this for?” “What must stay?” “What should I avoid?” “What format do you need?” Repeat the brief back and let A correct one detail.

Self-study: record A’s brief, then answer B’s questions out loud. You can complete every task without opening an AI tool.

Draft to reject · deliberately flawed practice copy

Erase wrinkles overnight with the world’s safest moisturiser. Half price, today only. Buy before it sells out!

Identify the unsupported promises, the incorrect offer, and the missing required CTA. Then respond to the two challenges below without approving invented facts.

“Those claims are stronger. Can’t we leave them in?”

“Fine. I’ll just tell the AI to make it better.”

Suggested responses
  1. Use the approved brief, and avoid claims we cannot support. Make sure it says ten percent off until Sunday and ends with “Shop the launch.”
  2. Revise the opening so that it fits a busy morning, while keeping the approved facts, the offer, and the exact call to action.

Round 2 · same campaign, a tighter brief

The team now needs each caption under 30 words, including the CTA. Keep all three variants, the correct offer, and the friendly voice.

Give a focused revision request. Your partner should challenge one detail, then repeat the updated brief. Swap roles and do a second two-minute round; use the remaining time to give feedback.

Across the two rounds, use all five chunks at least once. Tick a chunk only after you say it.

One possible revision request
Revise the opening so that it speaks to a busy morning, while keeping the approved facts, the ten percent offer, the Sunday end date, and the exact call to action. Keep each complete caption under thirty words. Return the same numbered list of three captions.

0 words · under 30 required

One acceptable sample caption
Busy morning? Try LumaAir’s lightweight, fragrance-free gel moisturiser. Get 10% off until Sunday. Shop the launch.

Other wording can work. Judge it against the brief, not against this exact sentence.
